01 / About

A clearer view of farmers’ priorities.

Rerouted Results gathers structured insight from farms and the wider agricultural sector to better understand what farmers value, what they are concerned about, and how they make decisions.

Our research looks beyond surface-level data. We focus on farmer interests, technology adoption, confidence, investment intentions, information sources and attitudes toward farming in Ireland.

02 / Research areas

What we study.

01

Farmer interests

We explore what matters to farmers across day-to-day farm management, future planning, business priorities, information needs and rural life.

02

Technology adoption

We examine how farmers view new technologies, what influences adoption, what creates hesitation, and which tools are seen as useful on farm.

03

Views on farming in Ireland

We capture farmer perspectives on change within Irish agriculture, including regulation, sustainability, succession, labour, costs and confidence in the sector.

High quality data = high quality results
